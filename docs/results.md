## Drift scenario

1. v1 trained on the initial period and promoted (no champion yet).
2. Drift injected on a share of machines: PSI above 0.2 on voltage and vibration, pressure under the threshold and not flagged.
3. Challenger v2 retrained on the recent window and compared with v1 on the same unseen window: hypothetical total cost 6.43 M€ → 4.24 M€ (−34 %), above the 2 % requirement, so promoted.
4. Control without drift: no action.

Decisions are in `logs/promotion_decisions.jsonl` and in the dashboard.
